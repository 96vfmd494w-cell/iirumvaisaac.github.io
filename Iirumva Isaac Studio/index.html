About

Artist statement

Manirumva Isaac,
known professionally as Iirumva Isaac.
Born 1998. Lives and works in Kigali, Rwanda.

Iirumva Isaac is a Rwandan contemporary visual artist whose figurative painting practice explores identity, memory, and human connection through everyday life and storytelling.

Rooted in lived experience and personal memory, his work focuses on relationships, shared spaces, and the quiet emotional moments that shape community life. Working primarily with painting, he uses expressive color and gesture to reveal intimacy within ordinary scenes.

His practice also includes photography, digital media, and experimental moving image. As an artist from the Global South, he is interested in how localized experiences of connection, love, and cultural negotiation resonate across different geographies, creating spaces for dialogue and cross-cultural exchange.

On the work

Iirumva Isaac’s work is ‘Connection made tangible’. His work explores people’s relationships, culture, and inevitable change. How can art show the bridging of gaps that are fraught with miscommunication and misunderstanding between different cultures, generations, and even lovers? Is identity defined through self, or the eyes of others?

Born in 1998, Isaac defines his art as 21st century contemporary, influenced by global cultures and technological advancement. With bright colors and vivid playful characters of a naïve style, Isaac immediately transports the viewer to a story in progress with compelling interactions. It is hard not to smile. The interiors are welcoming, not claustrophobic, and the titles give us a clue to the drama unfolding. You can almost hear the conversations taking place, told through a look or the placement of Isaac’s stylized characters. But peer closely and there is more ambiguity and layers to unwrap to understand what is really going on.

Looking at culture—both written and unwritten—over time, Isaac explores reaching across generational shifts, with the young wedded to technology and the old to tradition. How can they not just co-exist but nurture each other? Equally, in showing romantic love, his couples portrayed from Date Night to Marriage show affection but perhaps also humored frustration on the path to intimacy. Portraits of friends show camaraderie but also that bit of competition.

Through his choice of color, bold characterization and humorous titles, self-taught artist Isaac gives life to his thoughts and identity to his characters. He speaks for those who cannot speak for themselves and we are fortunate to get to know them through his art. He captures life’s exuberance and human interconnectedness with all its joys and foibles.

Iirumva Isaac currently lives and works in Kigali, Rwanda and was a finalist in the 2022 pan-African Emerging Painting Invitational.
